## 1. Verdict

The codebase is in good shape for a 0.1.0. The secrets story, the staged download commit, the one-writer contracts for `state.json` and the push queue, and the reducer-plus-harness pattern on the QML side all hold under adversarial reading. The two previous briefs' M-severity items are closed.

What this review adds is mostly at the edges of the main flows: a `cancel` that can kill the wrong process, a position push that can bypass the stale check, an mpv load failure the UI never notices, a setup path that deletes a working venv before it has a replacement, and a per-tick chapter re-derivation that will hurt on 300-chapter books. On the UX side the single most important item is that **download cancel is not wired to any button** even though the backend, the job queue and the CHANGELOG all say it exists; the second is that a stray Enter on a fresh Library picks row 0 (Chris hit this in R5).

Suggested gating for 0.1.0 was F40, F41, F43, UX1, UX3. 0.1.0 shipped the same evening with UX3 fixed and the other four open (see the update above); they are the 0.1.1 set.

| F41 | H | `commands.py:554-575` (`cmd_cancel`) | **`cancel` SIGTERMs whatever process now owns a stale `job.json` pid.** `job.json` is only removed by `job_lock`'s `finally` (`joblock.py:78-81`), which never runs after SIGKILL/OOM or a SIGTERM that lands between lock acquisition and handler installation. ## 4. Checked and fine (so nobody re-checks)

- **Key path** end to end (see §0). `play-info` is the single event that carries a key, by design; no `ffmpeg`/`ffprobe` argv anywhere carries key material; `key.json`/`auth.json`/`activation_bytes`/session files are `0600` via `os.open` or forced umask; `log.scrub` covers the F4 suffix set including `device_private_key`, `adp_token`, `website_cookies`, quoted-JSON and `bearer` shapes; the pasted URL goes stdin → `login_finish` → `del code` and `Onboarding._p.safeText` guarantees it is never echoed.
- **B14/F35 commit**: staged and probed inside `.partial/`, old copy moved aside not deleted and restored on any `BaseException`, audio rename last, `pthread_sigmask` genuinely blocks SIGTERM across the window, late cancel counts as success (modulo F44).
- **`remove`** is path-safe (`resolve()` + `parents` + symlink refusal); `atomic_write_*` are tmp+fsync+`os.replace`; the job lock is non-blocking `flock` in a `0700` runtime dir; `cli.main`'s top-level guard keeps stdout NDJSON on any bug.
- **state.json**: one writer, never written before a successful read, I/O failure leaves it unloaded with a 30 s retry, garbage backed up to `.corrupt`, pending ops replay on adopt, shutdown flush waits. Save/push dirty flags are separate and correct for the 10 s vs 60 s ticks; the reattach position is not pushed.
- **Auto-remove / removal**: `atEnd` double-checks path and position after 2 s, a loaded book always unloads first, `Unload.jobAllowed` re-gates at job start, `busyAsins` covers loaded/resuming/play-info/pending-load. No path removes a wrong book.
- **PlayerMachine**: attach/launch/reconnect/quit/relaunch probed including quit-while-launching then play, quit-while-attaching with no load, play while `lost`, stale socket at startup, scope that never goes. No dead end or double launch.
- **NDJSON / jobs**: chunk splitting, `\r`, trailing partial line flush, exit≠0 with/without terminal, busy retry once with the input dropped and `inputLost` fail-fast, FIFO with non-job bypass, re-entrant `pump` after `jobFinished`. `Sync.step` re-reads before every push, `flushPlan` honours `own`, `refused` handled re-entrantly.
- **Settings precedence** (§3) matches `Settings.speedChoice` across fresh/changed/unchanged; repeated `applySettings` is idempotent; speed labels are never string-compared in logic (`Player.isSpeed` is numeric, `speedLabel` uses `toFixed`).
- **Missing/garbage catalog.json** → no rows, `catalogLoaded` false, diagnostic state; **reattach to a live socket with another book** → `snapAsin` from the reported path, not pushed, `wanted` stays false, as §7 says.
- **Rule 5 (theme tokens)**: no `#hex`, no named colours except `"transparent"` and the mask source; one `Qt.rgba` (`SoftText`, documented); fonts and radii all tokens.
- **Done well in the UI**: the inline download question with a size estimate and Esc; masked paste field and clipboard-history notice; "Checking Audible…" in place of remaining time plus the jump note and `seekSettled`; tooltips that name the key; chapter list follow-with-hold and tick suppression above 40 chapters.
